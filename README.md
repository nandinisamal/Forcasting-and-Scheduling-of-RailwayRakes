
Forecasting & Scheduling Railway Rakes

A smart railway logistics management system developed to optimize coal transportation through efficient rake allocation and scheduling. The platform enables real-time coal stock monitoring, siding management, and intelligent rake placement recommendations to reduce demurrage charges and improve operational efficiency.

Key Features
-Real-time coal stock tracking
-Railway siding management
-Smart rake placement recommendations
-Demurrage charge monitoring
-User authentication and role management
-Android application with backend integration
Tech Stack
-Android
-PHP
-MySQL
-XAMPP
-Objective

To improve railway freight operations by ensuring rakes are dispatched to sidings with adequate coal stock, minimizing delays, reducing costs, and enhancing overall transportation efficiency.

Outcome

The system streamlines coal transportation, optimizes resource utilization, reduces idle time and demurrage charges, and supports reliable railway logistics operations.

Developed as a solution for efficient coal transportation and railway rake scheduling.
